# Hardware Overview

The AmazingHand system consists of mechanical and electronic components that work together to produce controlled finger movement.

---

## Mechanical Components

- Finger linkage system
- Joint assemblies
- Structural frame

---

## Actuators

- Servo motors used for finger movement
- Each finger is typically controlled independently

---

## Electronics

- Microcontroller (e.g., Arduino or equivalent)
- Power distribution system
- Signal wiring for servo control

---

## Assembly Overview

The mechanical structure and actuators are combined to form a dexterous robotic hand capable of controlled movement.
